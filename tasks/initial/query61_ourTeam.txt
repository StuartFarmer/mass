Build and evaluate a causal daily BTC/USD allocation policy equivalent to rsikit's BitcoinEnv. Maximize final USD cash minus initial cash after all trading fees over the training period. This is an offline historical simulation.

Exact environment contract:
- Reset to $10,000 USD cash and zero BTC. Cash earns no interest. No leverage, borrowing, shorts, external signals, or additional assets. Staying entirely in cash is valid.
- Each action is a float64 array [target_btc_fraction] in [0,1], measured AFTER fees. Reject invalid shapes, non-real values, nonfinite values, and out-of-range targets.
- The only observation is float64[5]: current BTC/USD price; last daily simple return (zero at reset); current BTC allocation before trading; current wealth / 10000; and Python date ordinal. The policy may retain past observations in memory, reset for each episode. It must not read the price file, future rows, or memorized date-to-action tables during decisions.
- Execute the trade at the currently observed price, then advance one day. These are idealized daily-price fills, with no extra spread or slippage in the primary benchmark. Every buy and sell costs 0.001 times its USD notional.
- Use exact self-financing accounting. For wealth W, current BTC fraction a, target t, and fee f, signed trade notional x = (t-a)*W / (1+f*t) when t >= a, otherwise x = (t-a)*W / (1-f*t). Fee = f*abs(x), funded wealth = W-fee. At the next price, BTC value = t*funded_wealth*(next_price/current_price), cash = (1-t)*funded_wealth. Recompute wealth and allocation from these values.
- After the final price advance, liquidate all BTC at that final price and charge the sell fee. There are N-1 decisions for N prices, with no extra decision at the terminal price. Reward is the change in USD wealth including trading and terminal liquidation fees; summed reward must equal final cash minus $10,000.
- Repeating a fixed target rebalances drift and can incur fees. Returning the observed current fraction holds without a trade. Reset always replays the same price path, independent of seed; repeated seeds are not independent market samples.
